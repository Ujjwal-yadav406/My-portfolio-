ABOUT US

Birgunj Neuro and General Hospital Research Center Pvt. Ltd.

Birgunj Neuro and General Hospital Research Center Pvt. Ltd. is a modern healthcare institution established in Birgunj, Parsa, dedicated to providing comprehensive medical services with a special focus on neurology and general medicine.

Our goal is to deliver high-quality, affordable and patient-centered care with the support of modern technology and research.
